# Get paid by agents [Agents are your newest customers]

Agents already pick and buy infrastructure on their own. They spin up databases, sandboxes and inference endpoints in the middle of a task, and a growing share of new signups on developer platforms never touches a signup form. MPP lets those agents pay you in the same HTTP request they use to call your service: no account, no API key, no card form.

You add one middleware to the routes you want to charge for. Agents that hit them get a `402` with your price, pay, and get the response.

## Why it's worth adding

- **Agents buy without signing up.** An agent with a Tempo wallet can pay any MPP service the first time it sees it. You don't build onboarding, issue keys or chase a human for a card.
- **Bill per token, row or second.** With [Sessions](/payment-methods/tempo/session) the agent locks up a deposit once and pays every request with a signed voucher your server checks in CPU time. You charge fractions of a cent per unit with no latency on the hot path and no prepaid credits to manage.
- **Agents become customers.** Every payment carries the payer's wallet, so the agent's work is attached to an identity from the first request. A human can later claim it and upgrade to a plan. See [Let agents sign up as guests](/get-paid/guest-accounts).
- **Get paid where you already get paid.** Payments land in your Stripe balance next to the rest of your revenue, or in a wallet you hold. See [payouts](/guides/integrate-your-service#how-you-get-paid).
- **Agents can fund with a card.** Agents top up with MACH, Tempo credit bought by card or Apple Pay, and you still receive stablecoins.

## Recommended setup

Lead with Tempo Sessions, accept MACH and OUSD first with USDC.e as fallback. That covers every Tempo wallet, including agents that only hold MACH. Add other [payment methods](/payment-methods), such as cards through Stripe, when you need payers outside Tempo.
